Is it magic or physical chemistry? In this experiment, we set up the Briggs–Rauscher reaction, a famous oscillating chemical clock, and sustained an incredible 24 full color transitions before the reactants depleted! 🧪✨

Most demonstrations stop around 10 to 15 oscillations, but optimal temperature, continuous magnetic stirring, and fresh reagents kept this chemical feedback loop running far longer than expected.
